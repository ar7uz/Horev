— About the Clinic —

A binational clinic, run with one standard of care.

HOREV Medical Center has been practising in Tashkent for more than thirty years. In 2006 the clinic took on international status through formal cooperation with the Horev Medical Center in Haifa, Israel — a research hospital with more than three hundred professors. Since then, our specialists have been trained on rotation in Israel, and Israeli professors hold master classes in our theatre several times a year.

Today the clinic operates sixteen departments — from cardiology and neurology to plastic surgery and dentistry — staffed by forty-one physicians, many holding the highest medical category. The in-patient ward runs twenty-four-hour monitoring; the surgical suite handles gynaecological, urological and aesthetic procedures under international safety protocols.

We chose to publish our consultation prices openly because trust, in medicine, begins with predictability. There are no hidden line items at HOREV. The number on the page is the number at the desk.
